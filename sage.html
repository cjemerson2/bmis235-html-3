Meet Sage!

She is the middle cat and is the sweetest cat you'll meet.

Personality

Sage is one of the sweetest cats you will ever meet! She loves to snuggle and will often meow and chortle for attention if you are not petting her. Sage has a habit of flopping on her back and showing her stomach to ask for pets and belly rubs. She will often curl up in oyur arms or lap and snuggle you or sleep under covers for hours on end.

Favorites

  • Favorite Sleeping Spots: Top of the rocking chair, cat trees, snuggling our dog, or snuggling someone.
  • Favorite Toy: Laser, toy on a string, tunnels
  • Favorite Person: Laura (my mom!)
